The Broker in a Hat

About Tara Hayes

Knoxville Realtor, broker, and licensed building contractor — since 2015.

Eleven years, two licenses, one hat

Tara Hayes has been selling East Tennessee real estate since August 2015 — a broker at Realty Executives Associates, a licensed Tennessee building contractor, and the easiest agent to spot at any closing: she's the one in the hat.

The track record keeps pace with the tenure: more than 215 closings and over $65 million in sales volume in the last five years alone, with repeat clients and their referrals making up much of her business.

The two licenses are the whole story. Most agents can tell you what a house is listed for; Tara can tell you what it's worth fixing. Buyers get an informal contractor's read on every showing. Sellers get pre-list advice on which repairs pay for themselves. New-construction clients get someone who reads plans and holds builders to them.

All of East Tennessee, one broker

Her practice covers the Knoxville metro and the communities around it — Farragut, Hardin Valley, Karns, Bearden, Maryville, Oak Ridge, Lenoir City, Tellico Village, and the lake country in between — with services spanning family homes and first purchases, luxury and lakefront, land, new construction, investing, and full-service property management.

Eleven years in, the reviews say the same thing over and over: honest, responsive, and knows houses. Read them, then call — Tara answers her own phone.
