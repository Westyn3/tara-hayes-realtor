Luxury and lakefront

Discreet, white-glove representation for East Tennessee's finest properties — in town and on the water.

East Tennessee's luxury market runs from estate homes in Farragut and Sequoyah Hills to lakefront on Fort Loudoun, Tellico, and Watts Bar. These transactions demand discretion, precise pricing, and a network — many of the best properties trade quietly.

Tara brings all three, plus something rare in the luxury space: a contractor's structural judgment on homes where a hidden problem carries six-figure consequences. Dock permits, seawalls, geothermal systems, whole-home generators — she evaluates the things that make lake property different.

Whether you're acquiring or quietly listing, conversations start confidential and stay that way.
