New construction, with a contractor watching

From lot selection to final walkthrough — representation backed by a licensed building contractor's eye.

The builder's sales agent works for the builder. Tara works for you — and she speaks the builder's language, because she holds a Tennessee contractor's license herself. That changes every stage of a new-construction purchase: reviewing the lot and site prep, reading the plans and allowances, catching corner-cutting at the pre-drywall walk, and holding the builder to the punch list at final walkthrough.

New construction is booming across Hardin Valley, Farragut, Maryville, and the surrounding counties. Tara knows the local builders — who builds tight and who builds fast — and negotiates upgrades, closing costs, and rate buydowns that buyers rarely get on their own.

Best of all: in most cases the builder pays your agent's commission, so this representation costs you nothing.
