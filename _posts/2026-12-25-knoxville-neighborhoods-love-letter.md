It's Christmas morning, so no market stats today — just gratitude for the place where I've spent eleven years helping families find home.

To **[Sequoyah Hills]({{ '/areas/sequoyah-hills.html' | relative_url }})**, whose boulevard in December looks like the inside of a snow globe, and whose houses have watched a century of Christmases.

To **[Bearden]({{ '/areas/bearden.html' | relative_url }})**, which taught Knoxville that you can walk to dinner, and where half my closings end with a celebration on Kingston Pike.

To **[Farragut]({{ '/areas/farragut.html' | relative_url }})**, the town that families drive across the country for, sight unseen, because the schools' reputation got there first. It earns it.

To **[Hardin Valley]({{ '/areas/hardin-valley.html' | relative_url }})**, where there's a street today that was a hayfield when I got licensed — and where half the porches have tricycles on them. Nothing makes me happier than a neighborhood full of first Christmases.

To **[South Knoxville]({{ '/areas/south-knoxville.html' | relative_url }})**, the comeback story — Urban Wilderness trails, river views, and the stubborn character that never left.

To **[Maryville]({{ '/areas/maryville.html' | relative_url }})** and **[Townsend]({{ '/areas/townsend.html' | relative_url }})**, who get the Smokies for a backyard and stay humble about it.

To **[Tellico Village]({{ '/areas/tellico-village.html' | relative_url }})**, proof that some of the best chapters start at 62, on a pontoon.

And to every family who handed me their keys-day photo this year — from first condos in [North Knoxville]({{ '/areas/north-knoxville.html' | relative_url }}) to lakefront on Fort Loudoun — thank you. This work is the best seat in town: I get to watch East Tennessee become home, one family at a time.

Merry Christmas, Knoxville. If 2027 is your year to find your place here, you know where to find me. 🎄

*— Tara, the Broker in the Hat*
