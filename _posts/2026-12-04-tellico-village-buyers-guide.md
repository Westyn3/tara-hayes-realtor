Every month, buyers from Florida, the Midwest, and the Northeast discover the same place: a master-planned lake community on Tellico Lake with three golf courses, multiple marinas, a yacht club, and homes that cost half what the equivalent costs on a Carolina lake. Here's the honest buyer's guide.

## What Tellico Village actually is

[Tellico Village]({{ '/areas/tellico-village.html' | relative_url }}) is a 4,800-acre planned community in Loudon County, about 45 minutes southwest of Knoxville. It's organized around the water and the amenities: Toqua, Kahite, and The Links golf courses, marinas and launch ramps, a wellness center, and more clubs than you'll have evenings.

## Who it fits

Mostly — though not only — active retirees and second-home buyers. The pace is resort-like, the neighbors are from everywhere, and the calendar is full. Families buy here too, particularly in Kahite where lots are larger and prices gentler.

## What it costs

The range is wide, which surprises people: interior homes from the $400s, golf-course homes in the $500s–$700s, and true lakefront from the $800s well into the millions depending on dock and view. Every property pays POA dues — budget for them, and ask me for the current schedule — which fund the amenities that make the Village work.

## What I check before you buy

Lake-community homes have their own diligence list, and this is my contractor's hat talking: dock permits and transferability (TVA rules apply), seawall condition, crawlspace moisture on lake-slope lots, and the age of big-ticket systems in homes built during the Village's 1990s–2000s growth spurts. On golf-course lots, I also check drainage — fairway-adjacent can mean water-adjacent in a storm.

## The honest trade-offs

You're 25–45 minutes from Knoxville proper, hospital access matters at this life stage (Loudon and Lenoir City cover the basics; UT Medical is the drive), and the POA structure means rules. Most Villagers consider all three a fair trade for the life.
