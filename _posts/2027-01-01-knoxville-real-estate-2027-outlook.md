Happy New Year, Knoxville. Every January I write down what I'm telling clients at kitchen tables, so here's the 2027 edition — observations from the ground, not a crystal ball.

## What carried over from 2026

Knoxville remains one of the Southeast's steadiest markets. In-migration hasn't slowed — the no-income-tax, lakes-and-mountains pitch keeps selling itself — and inventory, while better than the famine years, still runs short of demand in the family-favorite zones: [Farragut]({{ '/areas/farragut.html' | relative_url }}), [Hardin Valley]({{ '/areas/hardin-valley.html' | relative_url }}), [Maryville]({{ '/areas/maryville.html' | relative_url }}).

## What I'm watching in 2027

- **New construction pace.** Hardin Valley and [Powell]({{ '/areas/powell.html' | relative_url }}) keep delivering; if builder incentives stay generous, new builds remain the best-value lane for move-up families.
- **The luxury and lakefront lane.** Out-of-state wealth keeps discovering Tellico and Fort Loudoun. I expect waterfront to outperform the broader market again.
- **Rates.** Whatever they do, the lesson of the last three years stands: families who bought the right house when they found it are glad; families who timed the market are still renting.
