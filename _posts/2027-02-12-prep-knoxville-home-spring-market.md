Knoxville's spring market doesn't start in spring. The homes that launch strong in late March got their punch lists in February — and the sellers who wait until the dogwoods bloom spend their best weeks painting instead of fielding offers.

## Fix these — they return multiples

As a licensed contractor, I'll tell you the short list that actually pays. Water stains and their sources — nothing spooks a buyer faster, even when the leak was fixed years ago. Sticking doors, loose rails, dead outlets — small-stuff density is how buyers estimate how the big stuff was maintained. HVAC service with the sticker to prove it. Fresh neutral paint in the rooms that need it, and mulch-and-trim curb appeal, because the photo of your front elevation is your first showing.

## Skip these — they return almost nothing

The full kitchen remodel two months before listing — you'll spend $45,000 to pick finishes for a stranger who wanted different ones. Replacing a roof with honest years left instead of pricing it correctly. Any project that pushes your list date past peak season for marginal polish.
