[Oak Ridge]({{ '/areas/oak-ridge.html' | relative_url }}) was built in a hurry and in secret in the 1940s, and it's been quietly outperforming ever since. With ORNL and Y-12 hiring steadily and new development finally catching up, the Secret City is one of the best value plays in East Tennessee.

## Who's buying here

Lab and contractor employees, first and foremost — ten minutes to the plants beats forty from West Knoxville every day of the week. But I'm also seeing more Knoxville buyers priced out of Hardin Valley discovering that Oak Ridge delivers more house, an easier commute to the west side than they assumed, and Melton Hill Lake in the backyard.

## The housing stock, honestly

Oak Ridge is layered like an archaeology dig. The original wartime stock — cemestos, flat-tops — ranges from lovingly renovated to barely touched, and prices reflect it. Mid-century neighborhoods on the ridges bring big lots and views. And the newer construction along the Turnpike corridor and out toward Rarity Ridge competes head-on with Knox County while routinely costing less per square foot.

## The contractor's note

Those 1940s homes are the best and trickiest buys in town. Some have been fully modernized — new electrical, plumbing, insulation — and some have 80-year-old bones under cosmetic paint. I walk them with a contractor's eye: panel first, plumbing second, then the roofline and whatever the crawlspace wants to confess. Buy the renovated one, or buy the honest fixer at a fixer's price — just never pay renovated money for cosmetic work.
