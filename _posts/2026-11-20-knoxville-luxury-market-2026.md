Knoxville's luxury market doesn't behave like the rest of the market — different buyers, different pace, and a lot of it trades before a sign ever goes in the yard. Here's the honest tour.

## Where the seven-figure homes live

- **[Sequoyah Hills]({{ '/areas/sequoyah-hills.html' | relative_url }})** — the signature address. Historic estates along Cherokee Boulevard with the river at the end of the street. Scarcity is permanent; so is demand.
- **[Farragut]({{ '/areas/farragut.html' | relative_url }}) & gated West Knox** — newer estates, golf communities, and the county's deepest bench of $1M–$3M inventory.
- **Lakefront** — Fort Loudoun for in-town water, [Tellico]({{ '/areas/tellico-village.html' | relative_url }}) for resort living. Waterfront is its own currency: dock rights and shoreline quality move value as much as square footage.
- **[Townsend]({{ '/areas/townsend.html' | relative_url }}) & the foothills** — mountain-view estates and significant acreage on the quiet side of the Smokies.

## How luxury actually trades here

Quietly. A meaningful share of Knoxville's high-end sales never hit public MLS search — they move through agent networks, whispered listings, and buyers who registered their criteria with the right broker before the home existed as a listing. If you're only watching Zillow, you're seeing the luxury market's leftovers.
