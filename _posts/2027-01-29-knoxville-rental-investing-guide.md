Knoxville keeps showing up on national best-markets-for-investors lists, and the out-of-state calls keep proving it. Here's the local truth behind the headline: the numbers still work here, but not everywhere, and not at any price.

## Where the math still pencils

The steadiest cash flow in our market lives in the unglamorous middle: solid three-bedroom homes in [Powell]({{ '/areas/powell.html' | relative_url }}), [Halls]({{ '/areas/halls.html' | relative_url }}), Karns, and parts of North and South Knoxville, priced where rents cover the note with room to breathe. UT keeps the midtown rental market deep, the hospital systems and ORNL keep professional tenants coming, and our population growth shows no sign of slowing.

## Where it doesn't

Investors overpaying in appreciation-only neighborhoods and calling it cash flow. If the rent barely covers principal, interest, taxes, and insurance before maintenance and vacancy, you've bought a hope, not an asset.
