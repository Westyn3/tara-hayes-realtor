Spring is when Knoxville's market shows its whole personality: listings arrive in waves, open houses fill, and the best homes in the best school zones turn into weekend auctions. Here's how I'm coaching both sides of the table this season.

## If you're selling

The spring advantage is real but narrow — the strongest window is late March through May, before summer vacations thin the buyer pool. Launch ready: [prepped]({{ '/blog/prep-knoxville-home-spring-market/' | relative_url }}), photographed, and priced to the comps rather than to the neighbor's wishful list price. In a season this active, correct pricing doesn't leave money on the table — it's what creates the multiple-offer Saturday that takes the price past it.

## If you're buying

Spring means more choices and more competition arriving together. The buyers who win here do three things: get fully underwritten (not just pre-qualified) before the search, decide their real ceiling on a house before offer night rather than during it, and write clean offers — fewer contingencies they don't need, realistic dates, terms the seller's side can trust. Speed matters, but so does an agent who can tell you in the driveway what the house will need, which is [where I put my contractor's license to work]({{ '/blog/contractor-home-inspection-knoxville/' | relative_url }}).

## Both sides: watch the zone, not the headlines

National market stories are written about averages. Your street isn't average. Farragut, Powell, Oak Ridge, and the lake markets each run their own spring — I'll tell you what days-on-market and list-to-close ratios look like in yours this month, not last quarter.
