Commercial property

Representation for commercial buyers, sellers, and investors in the greater Knoxville area.

Knoxville's commercial market — retail, office, small multifamily, and mixed-use — rewards buyers who understand both the numbers and the building. Tara brings transaction experience on the brokerage side and construction fluency on the physical side: roof condition, mechanical systems, ADA considerations, and the true cost of a build-out.

  • Mixed-use buildings with commercial space below and residential above
  • Land and lots for future development; see land and building lots

Buying commercial property: what Tara checks

A commercial purchase is a numbers decision and a building decision. On the numbers side, Tara helps you look at rent rolls, leases, operating expenses and what the property really nets. On the building side, her contractor's license is a real advantage:

  • Roof age and condition, and remaining life
  • HVAC, electrical and plumbing capacity for how you plan to use the space
  • Accessibility (ADA) considerations and what upgrades may be needed
  • Zoning and permitted uses for your plans
  • The true cost and timeline of a build-out before you sign a lease or a contract

Selling or leasing a commercial property

Commercial buyers want clean numbers and a clear picture of the building. Tara helps sellers organize leases and financials, address the issues an inspection will raise, and position the property for the right buyer, whether that's an investor or an owner-occupant.
