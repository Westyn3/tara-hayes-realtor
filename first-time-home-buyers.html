Your first home, guided

Financing options, down payments, inspections, closing — Tara walks first-time buyers through every step.

Nobody is born knowing how earnest money works. Tara has guided first-time buyers through the Knoxville market for sixteen years, and the process is the same every time: explain everything, rush nothing, and make sure the first home is a good one.

You'll get plain-English guidance on loan types and down-payment assistance programs, what your monthly payment really includes, how inspections and appraisals work, and what happens at closing. And because Tara is a licensed contractor, she'll tell you honestly whether that 'starter home with potential' is a smart project or a money pit.

The first step is a conversation, not a commitment. Call or send a message and tell Tara where you are in the process.
